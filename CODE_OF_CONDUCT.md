# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple contributor covenant.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, but verify claims with files, diffs, tests, or docs.
- Preserve credit and license notices.
- Keep discussions useful for frontend developers and agent users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private credentials or personal information.
- Intentionally misleading benchmark, license, or security claims.
- Spam or unrelated promotion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may block users who repeatedly violate this code.
